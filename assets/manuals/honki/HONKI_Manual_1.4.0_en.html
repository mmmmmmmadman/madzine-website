1. Overview

HONKI is an eight-track phrase generator and sound source system for iPad. A pattern has 8 tracks, and each track has 8 segment memory slots.

The six page tabs in the top bar: MAIN, VOICE, MIXER, UTILITY, PERFORM, VISUAL. MAIN is the current page in the image.

Track structure

TRACK 1 to 4Melodic tracks. Notes are edited in the TRACK 1 to 4 editor on MAIN.
TRACK 5 to 8Drum tracks. Shown as FOUNDATION, GROOVE, TIMELINE, LEAD on MIXER and PERFORM. Edited in the DRUMS editor on MAIN.
Role pairingTRACK 1 and 5 are foundation, 2 and 6 groove, 3 and 7 timeline, 4 and 8 lead.
SegmentEach track has 8 segment slots. Three playback modes: SINGLE, SEQUENCER, RANDOM. Drum tracks also have FOLLOW.
Default pitch rangeMIDI pitch 36 to 72.
Sound sourceOne of four per track: SYNTH, SAMPLE, PHYSICAL, AU.

The six pages

MAINPhrase editing and segment management. The page shown at launch.
VOICESound source selection and parameters.
MIXEREight-track mixing, EQ, compression, effect sends, master effects.
UTILITY8-stage envelope, LFOs, CV output matrix.
PERFORMCustom control panel and mappings.
VISUALVideo source and four video processing stations.

To switch pages, tap a page tab in the top bar or press number keys 1 to 6 on an external keyboard. While an edit overlay is open, the number keys switch between TRACK 1 to 4 and DRUMS instead. Page switching can be assigned with MIDI Learn. Two-finger swipe for page switching has been removed.

Heading position

The square button at the right end of the top bar moves the heading between corners, in the order top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right. When the heading is at the bottom, the Transport bar moves to the top of the screen.

Project slots

Slot count26 banks (A to Z), 10 patterns per bank (00 to 09). Slot IDs use the format A-00.
Stored per slotbank, programs, modulation, PERFORM layout, MASTER FX, per-track AU.
App-wide (not per slot)INPUT COMP, HUE, CV device binding.
At launchRestores the last used slot.

Undo

Undo works on whole-bank snapshots, up to 100 steps. Multiple changes within the same tick merge into one step, and a drag merges into one step. MIDI Learn writes, TAP tempo and bank loading do not enter undo.

Lanes

VELOCITY1 to 127; shows "VELOCITY n" while dragging. New notes are 100.
PROBABILITY0 to 100%; shows "PROB nn%". Default 100%.
PITCH PROBABILITY0 to 100%; shows "PITCH nn%". The probability of a random pitch. Default 0.
DECAY0 to 100%; shows "DECAY nn%". Default 50%, which follows the DECAY of the segment; below 50% is shorter, above 50% is longer.
RATCHET0 to 100%; shows "RATCHET nn%". The probability that the step retriggers. Default 0.
RATCHET COUNTThe number of retriggers, nine values: 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32; shows "COUNT n". Default 2. For notes whose RATCHET is 0, the bar in this lane is faded but can still be edited.
RATCHET COUNT PROBABILITY0 to 100%; shows "COUNT PROB nn%". Default 0. When a RATCHET occurs, a second check is made: if it passes, the count becomes random, drawn from 1 and the values not exceeding the set COUNT, where 1 means the step does not retrigger; if it does not pass, the set COUNT is used.

All lanes support vertical drag and a one-finger horizontal sweep. The DECAY and RATCHET values of locked notes are not changed by generation.

TRI triplet grid

  • TRI only switches the grid used for display and editing. Straight notes and triplet notes are two separate sets of data; both play whether TRI is on or off.
  • On melody tracks, TRI applies only to the current track and is kept per track. The TRI state is not saved; it is off after the app restarts.
  • With TRI on, a bar changes from 16 columns to 24 columns: every two straight steps are divided into three cells. The first cell of each group is the same note as the even straight step.
  • With TRI on, notes on odd straight steps are shown at their original time position with a faded dashed frame and cannot be edited. With TRI off, triplet notes are shown with a faded dashed frame and cannot be edited.
  • Lanes available on triplet cells: VELOCITY, PROBABILITY, DECAY. PITCH PROBABILITY and the three RATCHET lanes do not apply to triplets; the lane shows "NOT AVAILABLE ON TRIPLET" at its top right, and the straight columns can still be edited.
  • A new triplet note has velocity 100 and is locked on creation.
  • Triplet notes are not affected by SWING and have no random pitch or retrigger. They are also silent under MUTE, SOLO and when DENSITY is 0.
  • Generation neither creates nor clears triplet notes. Deleting a bar deletes its triplets as well.
  • Triplets are stored with the segment and included in .honki files; files from older versions still load.

Note grid gestures

  • Rows are the scale notes within the pitch range; note width is gate and opacity is velocity.
  • Notes outside the scale show a 2pt warning frame; locked notes show a 4pt frame.
  • Tap an empty cell to create a note (velocity 100, gate 0.5), locked on creation. Tap a note to delete it; the lock state is kept.
  • Drag vertically more than 8pt to change pitch; drag horizontally to change gate (0.01 to 16). Up to 4 fingers at once. A drag that starts on an empty cell does not create a note.
  • Notes across bars: stretch a note to the right edge, hold until the page turns, and keep stretching on the next page. For a long note that starts on the previous page, the part that extends into this page is drawn from the left edge in a lighter colour.
  • In LOCK mode, notes can only be tapped to toggle their lock.

Manually entered notes are locked by default and are not affected by the LOCK knob or by generation. Locked notes are cleared only by choosing Y after CLEAR.

Each column of a parameter row is one bar whose height is the current value. Touching a column sets the value to the height of the finger: the top of the row is the maximum, the bottom is the minimum. 9. MOD ROUTE

MOD ROUTE lets one parameter receive several modulation sources at once. While a finger is on a control, the manual value replaces MOTION playback; MOD ROUTE keeps working and is added to MOTION. MOD ROUTE keeps working when playback is stopped.

Opening

  • Press MOD in the Transport to enter MOD mode. Controls that can be modulated show a solid accent outline.
  • Tap an outlined control to open the MOD ROUTE panel of that parameter, with haptic feedback. The value of the control does not change.
  • Press MOD again to leave. MOD mode and MIDI Learn mode are mutually exclusive; MOD is off every time the app launches.

The long-press method of older versions has been removed.

The MIXER page with MOD on in the transport. Every control that can be modulated gets an outline.

12. MIDI Learn

MIDI on in the transport. Every control that can be bound is marked with a dashed outline; the VOICE page is shown here.

Entering and leaving

  • Press MIDI in the Transport or Cmd+L to enter. Assignable controls show a dashed outline; the XY pad shows two corners, the top-left corner for the Y axis and the bottom-right corner for the X axis.
  • Press MIDI or Cmd+L again to leave; pressing MOD also leaves. ESC does not leave MIDI Learn mode.
  • Assignable targets added after 1.3.4: MIXER PAN, and for FX CHAIN the parameters, DRY WET, RETURN, the effect type (each trigger changes to the next), FREEZE and HOLD (active while held).

Procedure

  1. Tap a control to enter the waiting state; CURRENTLY LEARNING in SETUP shows the target.
  2. Send a MIDI message within 10 seconds. Tapping another control switches the target.
  3. The first matching message completes the binding: buttons are MOMENTARY, everything else is continuous, and the curve defaults to LINEAR. An existing binding is replaced.

Message types

BindableCC, Note, Pitch Bend.
Not bindableProgram Change. Aftertouch and SysEx are ignored.

Binding behavior

MODELATCH or MOMENTARY.
TAKEOVERJUMP jumps straight to the new value; PICKUP takes over only after passing the current value.
SCALELINEAR, EXP, LOG.
MIN / MAXMapping range.
Page switchingEach of the six pages can bind one message.

Bindings are stored in midi_mappings.json, with a .bak backup kept alongside. Each parameter can have only one binding.
