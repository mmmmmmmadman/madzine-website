Timbres are approximations in Web Audio (mallet-like melodic voices, synthesized kick, toms, snare, clap, hats and crash). HONKI's wavetables, drum synth and Turing machine variations are not reproduced; what you hear is exactly the written notes. The T2 delay knob plays track 2 later by that many milliseconds (shown also in sixteenth cells) and shifts it on the roll, so the delay-time phasing can be auditioned by ear.
